### 2026-09-20: Rate & Review Settings Action — Cross-Platform Implementation Contract
**By:** Gandalf (Lead)
**Requestor:** Jorge Balderas

## Placement (both platforms)
New section inserted **between "App Info" and "Onboarding"**, in that exact order, so it's visible without scrolling:
`Language → App Info → Rate & Review (NEW) → Onboarding → Support & Legal → Content Sources`

## Label / Icon / Accessibility (identical semantics both platforms)
- **Section header label:** "Rate & Review" (localized key, add to both `AppStrings` maps: EN "Rate & Review" / ES "Calificar y Reseñar")
- **Icon:** star — iOS SF Symbol `star.fill`, Android `Icons.Default.Star`
- **Row label:** "Rate Confirmation Saints" (EN) / "Calificar Confirmation Saints" (ES)
- **Footer copy:** "Enjoying the app? A quick rating helps other families find it." (localized)
- **Accessibility label:** "Rate Confirmation Saints in the App Store" (iOS) / "... in Google Play" (Android)
- **Accessibility hint/trait:** Button role (iOS `.isButton`; Android `Role.Button` via `clickable`)

## iOS Behavior
- Do NOT use `SKStoreReviewController`/`AppStore.requestReview` for this explicit button (throttled, unreliable for a deliberate tap). Open the direct App Store write-review deep link `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762463641?action=write-review` via `openURL`/`Link`. Centralize App ID as `AppStoreConfig.appID`.
- Fallback if URL fails: generic App Store search `https://apps.apple.com/search?term=Confirmation%20Saints`.

## Android Behavior
- Use Google Play In-App Review API (`review-ktx`) via `ReviewManagerFactory`, invoked from the Composable (not ViewModel, since ViewModel shouldn't hold an `Activity`).
- Known dev/emulator limitation: silently no-ops without Play Store/Play-signed install.
- Mandatory fallback: on throw or `BuildConfig.DEBUG`, open `market://details?id=com.yortch.confirmationsaints`, falling back to `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yortch.confirmationsaints`.

## Status
Contract approved by Gandalf; implementation/test coverage tracked separately (see Rate & Review test coverage decision below).
